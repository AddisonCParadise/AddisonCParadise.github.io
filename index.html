About

Hi, I'm Addison — an analytical, detail-oriented Business Analytics graduate student at Wake Forest University, building on a Business Analytics degree and International Business minor from the University of Tennessee's Haslam College of Business. I work in SQL, Python, R, Tableau, and Power BI to turn complex, messy data into insights teams can actually act on.

My "data identity" started with a much less technical skill: leading people. Directing programs for 400+ campers and a 75-person staff taught me how to bring structure to ambiguity fast — a mindset I now apply every time I open a new dataset. I care as much about the story a dashboard tells as I do about the model behind it, and I like sitting at the intersection of technical rigor and clear business communication.

I'm currently exploring full-time roles in analytics, strategy, and operations, and I'm always glad to connect with people working in these spaces.
